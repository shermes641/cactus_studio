🌵 The Cactus Studio Guide

Welcome to our digital nursery! We specialize in rare and exotic cacti. This guide will help you navigate our collection, manage your account, and ensure your new plants arrive safely.

🔑 Account Management

Registration & Verification

To purchase, you must create an account. Click "Register here" on the login screen. You will need to provide a valid email and a Costa Rican shipping address. After registering, check your email for a verification link to activate your account.

Profile & Security

Access your profile via the menu (☰) > "Profile". Here you can update your shipping address or change your password. We recommend a strong password with at least 8 characters, including numbers and symbols.

Order History

You can view all your past purchases in the Profile section. Click on any order to see the specific plants included, payment status, and shipping details.

🔍 Finding Plants

Navigation

Use the controls at the top of the page to navigate through our inventory. You can switch pages or change the number of items displayed per page (5, 10, or 20) to suit your browsing preference.

Search & Filters

Looking for something specific? Use the search bar to find cacti by common name, scientific name, or SKU. You can also filter the gallery by genus (e.g., Astrophytum, Mammillaria) using the category dropdown.

Plant Details

Click on any cactus image to view it in full-screen high resolution. We provide detailed scientific names to help collectors identify specific species.

🛒 Purchasing

Cart & Reservations

Clicking "Add to Cart" temporarily reserves the item for you. However, items are not permanently yours until checkout is complete. If an item sells out while in your cart, you will be notified before payment.

Payment Methods

We accept PayPal for international and automated payments. For local customers in Costa Rica, we offer a Manual Payment option (SINPE Móvil / Bank Transfer). Select "Other Payment" at checkout and upload your receipt.

Support

Need help? Open the menu (☰) and click "Contact Us" to send a message directly to our support team. We usually respond within 24 hours.
