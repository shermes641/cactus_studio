🌵 The Cactus Studio Guide

Welcome to our digital nursery! We specialize in rare and exotic cacti. Here is how to make the most of your visit.

🔑 Getting Started

  • Login: Use your WhatsApp number to log in. This creates a personal session so your cart is saved even if you leave the page.
  • Privacy: We use your number solely to identify your cart session locally.

🔍 Browsing the Collection

  • Navigation: Use the page numbers at the bottom to browse our extensive inventory.
  • Zoom: Click on any cactus image to open a full-screen view. You can add the item to your cart directly from this view.
  • Details: We list the common name and, where available, the scientific name for enthusiasts.

🛒 Shopping & Cart Behavior

  • Exclusive Reservation: When you click "Add to Cart", the item is removed from the main gallery. It is reserved specifically for you in your cart.
  • Managing Cart: Click the "Cart" button in the top right to view your reserved items.
  • Removing Items: If you remove an item from your cart, it immediately returns to the main gallery for others to see.
  • Checkout: Ready to buy? Open the cart and click "Proceed to Checkout".

❓ Troubleshooting

  • "Where did the cactus go?" If you added it to your cart, it's safe there! Check the cart sidebar.
  • "Can I clear my cart?" Yes, use the red "Remove All" button in the cart sidebar to release all items back to the inventory.
  • Logout: Click the logout button in the header to end your session.

Thank you for visiting The Cactus Studio.
