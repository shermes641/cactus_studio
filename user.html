🌵 The Cactus Studio Guide

Welcome to our digital nursery! We specialize in rare and exotic cacti. Here is how to make the most of your visit.

🔑 Account & Menu

Registration

New users can register for an account. You must enter a valid Costa Rican shipping address. You will need to verify your email address via a link sent to your inbox.

Login

Access your account with your email and password to save your cart and shipping details.

Forgot Password

If you cannot remember your password, click "Forgot Password?" on the login screen to receive a reset link via email.

Profile

Access your profile via the menu (☰) to update your shipping address or change your password.

Orders

View your purchase history via the menu (☰). Click on any order to see the specific items included in that order.

Contact Us

Need help? Use the 'Contact Us' button in the menu (☰), or simply click the Cactus Studio logo to send us a message.

🔍 Browsing the Collection

Navigation

Use the page numbers at the top to browse our extensive inventory.

View Options

Use the selector at the top of the page to change the number of items displayed per page.

Search

Looking for something specific? Use the search bar in the filter area to find cacti by name. Matches against Name, Price, and SKU are highlighted.

Zoom

Click on any cactus image to open a full-screen view. You can add the item to your cart directly from this view.

Details

We list the common name and, where available, the scientific name for enthusiasts.

🛒 Shopping & Cart Behavior

Item Availability

When you click "Add to Cart", the item is removed from the main gallery. However, if someone else buys it before you, you will be notified at checkout that it is not available.

Managing Cart

Click the "Cart" button in the top right to view your reserved items.

Removing Items

If you remove an item from your cart, it immediately returns to the main gallery for others to see.

Minimum Order

We have a minimum order amount. The cart will notify you if your subtotal is too low to proceed.

Checkout & Shipping

Ready to buy? Open the cart. You can update your shipping address here, but it must be a valid Costa Rican address. Click "Checkout" for card payments or "Other Payment" for SINPE/Transfer.

❓ Troubleshooting

"Where did the cactus go?"

If you added it to your cart, it's safe there! Check the cart sidebar.

"Can I clear my cart?"

Yes, use the red "Remove All" button in the cart sidebar to release all items back to the inventory.

Logout

Click the logout button in the menu (☰) to end your session.

Thank you for visiting The Cactus Studio.
